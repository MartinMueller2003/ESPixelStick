Monster Permanent Configuration

Each physical module uses three input channels: red, green, blue. Neutral white (R = G = B) drives cool white at that intensity, with RGB off. Colored RGB values stay unchanged with both white channels off. Warm white is always off.

The controller always sends R, G, B, CW, WW, 00 (48 bits), MSB first at 800 kHz, with a LOW reset of at least 80 µs. The reserved byte is always zero.
